            How it works

            1. 01

              The claim

              Reads the Amazon.in listing's price and its struck-through M.R.P.

              amazon_product
            2. 02

              The market

              Finds Indian stores selling the same model today.

              google_shopping google google_immersive_product
            3. 03

              The match

              Same model and memory, new, in stock, sold in India, within the M.R.P. Accessories, parts, EMI and import listings are dropped.

              same-model rules
            4. 04

              The verdict

              The median of other sellers is the street price. A big "% off" that saves nothing against it is measured from a price nobody charges.

              median · thresholds
