          How it works

          1

          The claim

          amazon_product reads the listing's price and its struck-through M.R.P.

          2

          The market

          google_shopping, google search and google_immersive_product find Indian stores selling it.

          3

          The match

          Same model, same memory, new, in stock, sold in India, priced within the M.R.P. Accessories, parts, EMI listings and import resellers are dropped.

          4

          The verdict

          The median of other sellers is the street price. A big "% off" that saves nothing against it is measured from a price nobody charges.
