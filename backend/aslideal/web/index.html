      How it works

      1. The claim. SerpApi's amazon_product engine reads the listing's price and its struck-through M.R.P.
      2. The market. google_shopping, google web search and google_immersive_product find every Indian store selling the same model.
      3. The match. Listings count only if they're the same model and memory, new, in stock, sold in India and priced within the M.R.P. Accessories, spare parts, EMI listings and import resellers are left out.
      4. The verdict. The median of the other sellers is the street price. A big "% off" that saves nothing against the street price is measured from a price nobody charges.
